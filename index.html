Areeb Ahmad Chaudhry

I'm an AI engineer at GoSaaS. I received my Bachelor of Software Engineering from NUST SEECS, where I worked with Dr. Nazia Perwaiz in the MachVIS Lab.

My research lies in multimodal learning, LLM agents and trustworthy AI: making models ground what they say in real evidence, and measuring when they fail. I've worked on anatomy-grounded radiology report generation [1] and parameter-efficient 3D multimodal LLMs for CT [2]. In industry, I've reduced hallucinations in a production voice agent and built evaluation pipelines for multi-agent LLM systems [more].

I am looking for PhD positions starting Fall 2027.
